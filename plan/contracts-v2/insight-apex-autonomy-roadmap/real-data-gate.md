# Real Data Gate — First Production Contract

## Purpose

Prove the full Insight Apex contract lifecycle with one truthful real-world contract before expanding intelligence or autonomy.

This is a validation gate, not a new architecture phase.

## Preconditions

- Phase 7.6 complete;
- production organization clean;
- Anthropic production credits/configuration operational;
- AI Gateway smoke test passed;
- original contract PDF available;
- authorized human reviewer identified.

## Flow

```text
Original PDF
  ↓
Contract born UNCLASSIFIED
  ↓
AI extraction proposal
  ↓
Evidence / page / excerpt / confidence
  ↓
Human review
  ↓
Canonical structured contract
  ↓
Governance classification → LIVE
  ↓
Party linkage
  ↓
Project linkage
  ↓
Obligations
  ↓
Measurement requirements
  ↓
Billing conditions
```

## Truth rules

- do not convert an existing demo contract into live;
- do not invent missing values;
- missing information remains `UNKNOWN`, `UNCLASSIFIED`, `NOT_CONFIGURED`, or equivalent;
- AI proposal is not contract truth;
- source PDF remains immutable original evidence;
- all accepted structured facts retain provenance.

## Validation areas

- Parties and roles;
- contract effective/expiry dates;
- values/currency;
- clauses;
- amendments if present;
- obligations;
- guarantees;
- insurance;
- indexation;
- measurement requirements;
- billing conditions;
- project linkage;
- audit history;
- AI analysis provenance.

## Gate

The gate passes only when:
- one real contract is onboarded end-to-end;
- no demo data is involved;
- no field is fabricated to satisfy UI;
- AI extraction has evidence;
- human review history exists;
- lifecycle transitions are auditable;
- contract can participate in Projects/Measurements without cross-domain writes;
- no production clean-slate invariant is broken.

## Complexity

**Low–Medium.**

Technically simple compared with later phases; operationally important because it is the first contact with real truth.

## Recommended models

**Primary operator:** Gemini 3.8 Flash or Claude Sonnet 5.

Why:
- mostly validation, navigation, evidence comparison, and bounded corrections;
- the architecture should already be frozen.

**Escalate to GPT-5.6 Sol High or Opus 5 only if the real contract reveals a domain-model defect.**

This should not become another broad architecture audit.
